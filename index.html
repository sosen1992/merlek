Privacy Policy for Mer Lek

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Welcome to Mer Lek ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding any data processed through our mobile application. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you access and use our service.

1. Information We Process

  • Numerology & Astrology Inputs: Phone numbers, dates of birth, or custom values submitted for calculation are processed strictly in real-time. We do not permanently record, log, or sell any numbers you enter.
  • Sim Shop Orders: If you place an order for a SIM card, minimal contact details (name and phone number) are collected solely to deliver the SIM and notify our team.
  • Technical Identifiers: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and device metrics (e.g., operating system version, Advertising ID) may be processed by integrated third-party SDKs.

2. Third-Party Integrations & Ads

Our application integrates trusted third-party partners to support core functionality and ad monetization:

  • Mobile Ad Networks (AppLovin MAX, Pangle, Unity Ads): Used to deliver rewarded video and interstitial ads. These networks may process advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA) to combat fraudulent traffic and serve relevant ads.
  • Firebase Firestore: Securely hosts SIM shop catalog listings, manages incoming orders, and anonymously caches numerology analysis results for performance optimization.
  • Telegram Bot API: Dispatches internal operational notifications for orders placed in the SIM shop.
  • AI Numerology Engine (DeepInfra): Generates automated interpretation responses in real-time via the DeepInfra platform without storing user identity.

3. Data Protection & Retention

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to external third parties.
  • All communications with external APIs are secured using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Contact information obtained from SIM shop purchases is retained only as long as necessary to complete delivery and support.
  • Numerology inputs and calculations are transmitted and cached anonymously, without ever being linked to your personal identity.

4. Children's Privacy

Mer Lek does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If we discover that any child's data has been transmitted inadvertently, we will immediately remove it.
